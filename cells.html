01 CELLS

Therapy Space for CELLS Recovery

Concept
Therapy
Type
Rest Area
Year
2024

CELLS is a therapy space designed to allow tired
urbanites to pause and recover themselves.

Here, visitors become “living cells,” connecting with one another, healing, and generating a continuous cycle of regeneration. Located on Nodeul Island at the heart of the Han River, the space offers sensory rest and emotional recovery at the intersection of nature and the city. Within a water-based ecological environment, visitors reconnect with themselves, engage with the space, and regain their senses. Rather than a fixed structure, it functions as a living organism—constantly evolving and renewing itself through human presence and relationships.
