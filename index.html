Seojin Park is a spatial design student at Hongik University and currently an exchange student at HSLU. She starts with a concept or a story and translates it into a spatial experience through her own interpretation.

Rather than following one fixed style, she looks for the spatial language that best expresses each story or identity. Her goal is to create spaces that communicate visually, evoke emotion, and leave a lasting impression.

Her work focuses on building a distinct world and atmosphere for each project. Through form, material, light, color, and visual composition, she explores how the identity of a concept can become a physical and visual environment.
