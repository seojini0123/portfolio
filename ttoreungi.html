07 ttoreungi

A picnic-style public space that offers shelter

Concept
REgeneration REcovery
Type
Rest Area
Year
2026

ttoreugi is a public shelter for moments of rest within the cycling flow of the Han River.

It expands these pauses into spatial experiences, connecting movement, rest, and both personal and public moments into a single continuous flow. Rather than interrupting movement, ttoreugi offers time to recover before the next start—transforming cycling along the Han River into a rhythm of restoration and renewal, and adding a new cadence to urban daily life.
